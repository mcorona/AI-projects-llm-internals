## Decision: when a managed RAG pays off and when an own pipeline does

| Client criterion | Better choice | Evidence from this week |
|---|---|---|
| **Quality** on a small corpus with high answer accuracy needs | Own pipeline, or a managed index fed with your own chunks | Own pipeline 1.000. With the own chunks, Bedrock and Vertex reach 0.985; from original files, 0.92 to 0.97 |
| **Ranking quality** without building a reranker | **Vertex with its semantic ranker** | nDCG@10 0.983 / 0.971 and MRR 0.977 / 0.962, above the own Haiku reranking, at 0.7 s instead of 2.3 s |
| **Mixed-format documents** (scans, charts, plans) without your own extraction step | **Bedrock BDA multimodal + RetrieveAndGenerate** | 0.969 from original files. The default Bedrock parser loses every image; Vertex OCR cannot read charts |
| **Lowest cost** at any volume | **Bedrock `Retrieve` + your own generator** | $0.00027 per answer, ≈ $0 fixed: cheaper than the hosted own pipeline at every volume |
| **Low to medium volume** (under ≈ 10,000 queries a month) | Any managed option | No fixed cost; Vertex search is free up to 10,000 a month |
| **High volume** (over ≈ 12,000 to 40,000 queries a month) | Own pipeline or Bedrock `Retrieve` | Vertex costs about 6 times the own pipeline at 100,000 a month; RetrieveAndGenerate with Haiku 4.5 costs more from ≈ 37,000 |
| **Latency** | Bedrock RetrieveAndGenerate | 1.5 s p50 for the full answer, in one call inside AWS |
| **Operation** (no database, index or reranker to run) | Managed | Creation, ingestion and deletion are scripted (`infra/`); there is no database to patch or size |
| **Security and multitenancy** | Either (neither is ruled out) | Both filter by tenant and role inside the query: leakage 0 in 672 queries |
| **Data residency** | Depends on the region the client needs | Bedrock and S3 Vectors are regional (us-east-1 here); this Vertex setup used `global` (it also offers `us` and `eu`). The own pipeline runs wherever it is deployed, including on-premises |
| **Provider lock-in** | Own pipeline | Each managed service has its own API, filter syntax, metadata format and parsers, and ingestion needed per-service format work (Markdown to HTML, images to PDF, Marketplace subscriptions). The own pipeline moves anywhere PostgreSQL runs |
| **Model choice** | Own pipeline or Bedrock `Retrieve` with your generator | RetrieveAndGenerate is limited to the account's enabled Bedrock models: Haiku 5.5 was blocked by quota here |

**Recommendation for this kind of client** (document assistant, a few thousand to tens of thousands of queries a month, mixed formats, strict multitenancy):

1. **Keep the own ingestion (chunking and extraction) and a single generator under your control.**
2. **For the index and ranking, use a managed service only where it wins on its own merits:**
   - Vertex's semantic ranker for ranking quality below about 10,000 queries a month.
   - Bedrock `Retrieve` for cost at any volume.
3. **Use a fully managed RAG** (Bedrock BDA + RetrieveAndGenerate) when the client cannot maintain an extraction pipeline for scans and images. Accept its higher cost per answer and its dependence on the models the account has enabled.

The own pipeline remains the reference for accuracy and portability. Its main weaknesses are the latency of LLM reranking and the fixed cost of hosting it.
