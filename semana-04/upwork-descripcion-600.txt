Before recommending fine-tuning, I measured it. On a synthetic legacy billing schema (PostgreSQL) with 46 natural-language questions, scored by exact result match: 27B model 45.7%; with business rules in the prompt 50.0%; with rules retrieved per question (RAG) 41.3%; 7B base 37.0%; 7B + LoRA 37.0%. The fine-tune changed the SQL, not the correct answers, so the decision was: the prompt is enough.
